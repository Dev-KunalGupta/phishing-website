About PhishGuard AI

PhishGuard AI is a hybrid phishing detection platform designed to protect users from malicious websites, fraudulent QR codes, and phishing-based email attacks.

Our system combines Machine Learning (XGBoost) with expert-driven rule intelligence to generate real-time risk scores (0–100) and provide explainable security insights.
